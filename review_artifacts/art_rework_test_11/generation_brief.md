# Test 11 Daytime Generation Brief

## Shared instruction

Use the Test 04 image as the scene and composition master. Use the corresponding calibration reference as the approved daytime rendering and quality reference.

Create an entirely new full-color daytime painting of the same recognizable environment. Do not recolor, brighten or retain the gradients of the nighttime image. Preserve the wide camera angle, major architecture, door and window positions, major object footprints, empty gameplay areas and overlay anchor spaces.

Render polished high-resolution pixel art with crisp readable pixel clusters, clean simplified city-pop forms and rich but controlled detail. Use natural late-morning light, soft shadows and unrestricted local material colors. Individual objects should retain their own believable colors; the scene must not receive a global blue, pink, yellow or brown tint.

Avoid night skies, neon glow, magenta-blue washes, added characters, UI, table icons, labels, text and watermarks.

## Calibration routing

- Apartment reference: residential interiors, motel spaces, house and corner store.
- Bar reference: bar, jazz club, lounge, pawn shop, underground casino, alley and private casino rooms.
- Grand Casino reference: coastal environments, boat casino, public casino areas and glass-fronted resort spaces.

Individual scene prompts additionally identify the major features that must remain recognizable in each environment.
