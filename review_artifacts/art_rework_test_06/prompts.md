# Test 06 Art Brief

## Noon master

Preserve the supplied composition, camera, architecture, openings, platform edges, interaction bays, object staging zones, and gameplay-safe negative space. Return the scene to a deliberate pixel-art aesthetic while keeping the simplified city-pop architectural direction.

Construct the image on a visible 450 × 215 logical grid for exact 2× nearest-neighbor scaling to 900 × 430. Use hard one-pixel edges, compact pixel clusters, flat color blocks, and a 32–48-color visual vocabulary. Avoid antialiasing, smooth gradients, brush texture, photoreal materials, bloom, and 3D-render shading.

Use clear but restrained midday lighting. The scene should be medium-value and sun-warmed, never white, washed out, or globally tinted. Preserve local material colors. Limit each major material to one base, one highlight, and one shadow.

Keep every spatial landmark and gameplay slot aligned. Do not add or remove props. Do not bake runtime characters, labels, games, cards, chips, icons, or text into the background.

## Midnight derivation

Use the approved noon image as the immutable geometry master. Change only palette and lighting:

- cool large shadow masses toward deep navy or aubergine
- keep local hues recognizable at lower values
- deepen outdoor windows, sky, and water
- strengthen selected amber, cyan, coral, and magenta practical lights
- avoid a universal two-color grade
- maintain the same 450 × 215 logical pixels
- quantize to no more than 48 colors without dithering

The noon and midnight versions must be pixel-aligned for shader interpolation.
