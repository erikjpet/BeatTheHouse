## Geometry note

The current noon art and Test 04 night art were created in separate passes. They are compositionally similar but not pixel-identical. They are suitable for evaluating the two endpoint styles, but a direct long crossfade would expose small architectural differences.

For production animation, Test 04 should become the locked geometry master. The final noon, dawn, morning, golden-hour, and dusk states should be lighting edits of that exact master.
