# Corrected Midnight Direction

Use the approved noon endpoint as immutable geometry. Change lighting and palette only.

Create a genuinely dark midnight atmosphere. Most of the environment should be near-black navy, ink blue, aubergine, burgundy, or dark local material color, interrupted by small concentrated neon and practical-light sources.

Use an original late-1980s coastal city-pop and Miami-night visual language: serene graphic architecture, large shadow masses, deep negative space, hot pink and magenta neon, electric cyan edge light, coral spill, and occasional amber lamps. Blue supports the shadows; it must not become the dominant visible surface color.

Preserve recognizable local colors in illuminated materials. Wood can remain dark natural brown, plants dark green, upholstery burgundy or jade, brass amber, and cream architecture warm gray. Do not apply a universal blue or two-color grade.

Keep the 450 × 215 logical pixel grid, hard clusters, flat stepped lighting, and 48-color maximum. Avoid smooth gradients, bright cyan skies, teal floors, global bloom, detailed texture, and independently redrawn geometry.

No added props, signs, text, characters, machines, game objects, labels, cards, chips, watermarks, or borders.
