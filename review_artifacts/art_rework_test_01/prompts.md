# Generation Briefs

All assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Shared direction: original retro coastal 1980s pop-art mood, crisp geometric forms, saturated cobalt/coral/ivory color, hard clean light, no text, no logos, no watermark, and no imitation of any specific artist.

## Environments

### The Beach

Wide game-environment concept of a sunlit seaside boardwalk beside a turquoise pool and calm ocean, low white modernist motel architecture, palms, striped umbrellas, a small gaming kiosk, and open paths for character navigation. Elevated three-quarter view under hard midday sunlight with long cobalt shadows.

### Jazz Club

Wide game-environment concept of an intimate sunken lounge with curved white banquettes, a tiny empty trio stage, geometric cocktail and card tables, and windows onto a moonlit pool and palms. Elevated three-quarter view with cobalt night, warm coral and amber interior light, and slight noir tension.

### Grand Casino Main Floor

Wide game-environment concept of a monumental casino inside a white modernist hotel, with broad navigation lanes, table games, a sculptural stair, cashier cage, restrained slot banks, and ocean windows at sunset. Elevated three-quarter view with golden-hour light crossing a cool blue interior.

## Items

Each item is a centered square inventory illustration on pale ivory with a crisp cobalt cast shadow, generous padding, and a bold silhouette designed to remain readable at small card scale.

- **Cheap Sunglasses:** black plastic frames, cobalt lenses, coral horizon reflection.
- **Thermos of Black Coffee:** worn navy body, coral cup-cap, small steel accents.
- **Marked Cards:** five face-down navy cards with a coral sun motif and tiny gold cheat marks.
- **Lucky Keychain:** brass motel key and coral fob embossed with a sun-and-wave symbol.
- **Neon Player's Charm:** cobalt acrylic sunset arch with coral and gold bands and a suspended chrome token.

## Character

### Pal

Full-body model sheet showing the same character from the front, three-quarter, and side. Medium-brown skin, dark hair under a navy cap, teal resort jacket, ivory open-collar shirt, coral accent, navy trousers, and white shoes. Friendly, alert, relaxed, and slightly roguish. Existing character-data colors were used as the base palette.
