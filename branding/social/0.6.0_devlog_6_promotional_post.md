# Beat the House 0.6 promotional post

## Caption

log #6 — the town remembers tonight

Version 0.6 turns each visit into a situation, not just a room. Fifty-five
persistent Living Town scenarios bring their own actors, props, objectives,
actions, phases, and aftermath—and the town remembers what happened when you
leave, save, and return.

A wedding charter can take over the Delta Queen. A storm can roll across the
beach. An audit can lock down the Grand Casino floor. Each night changes what
you see, who is there, what you can do, and what may be left behind afterward.

Scratch Tickets also have a new home: the fully rebuilt Lucky Road vending
machine. Pick from seven stocked ticket rows, watch the lift shelf fetch and
lower your choice with machine audio, collect it from the delivery tray, and
move it onto the play surface. Stock returns on a deterministic three-hour
in-game cycle, and a scalper encounter may tip you off about the next drop.

What's new:

- 55 persistent Living Town scenarios across the town
- unique scenario actors, props, objectives, choices, phases, and aftermath
- visible consequences that survive travel, save/load, and return visits
- rebuilt physical Scratch Ticket vending cabinet and delivery sequence
- seven distinct Scratch Ticket games with stocked rows and timed restocking
- new Crew heist ending at the Grand Casino
- Coin Pusher, back-room Hold'em, and Craps expand the 11-game lineup
- new weather, rumors, schedules, and Police Sweeps
- deeper game interactions, NPC behavior, conversations, and poker tells
- expanded guided tutorial, adaptive music, surface SFX, and run reports
- major animation, loading, performance, balance, and polish work

Eleven games. One living town. The house remembers tonight.

Beat the House is a single-player game with simulated gambling only. It has no
real-money wagering, cash prizes, or gambling monetization.
