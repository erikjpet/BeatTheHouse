Updated 2026-11-12. Five main scene PNGs and five matching neighborhood backdrops were edited with the built-in image generation tool from the existing admired venue artwork. Final PNGs are exact returned bytes, with no raster cropping, resizing, alpha rewriting or recompression.

## Layer contract

Each main RGBA PNG contains the original destination facade and its own continuously painted extended ground. Sky and far-neighborhood areas have been removed. Gas retains concrete fuel apron and open transparency between canopy/pumps; other destinations retain their own urban asphalt. Clear travel lanes have no stairs, vegetation, planters, fountains or crossing sidewalks. Backdrops are opaque RGB continuations of the corresponding original neighborhood, with the main destination and foreground ground removed. The Punchline remains the public comedy club, not an invented underground casino facade.

All main images are 1774 × 887. Backdrops are 1774 × 887 except The Punchline at its native 1773 × 887. Use each backdrop texture's native size when fitting it. Both walking and vehicle modes should use the same main scene crop, without a separate ground plane or mode-specific zoom.

Generated matte values are not binary: sampled solid door/road interiors are alpha251–254, and removed sky/gas canopy gaps are alpha0–1. Most removed sky pixels are exactly zero. Preserve these source bytes. Runtime cutout compositing promotes main-scene alpha>=0.80 to1 and suppresses alpha<=2/255 while preserving intermediate silhouette coverage; vehicle alpha uses its separate0.95 threshold. No painted checkerboard or visible distant city remains in the main layer.
