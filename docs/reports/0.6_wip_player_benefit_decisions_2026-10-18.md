## Why the scenario moves are superseded

`DeveloperPlacementStore._room_slot_overrides` merges the exact scenario's
position overrides with replacement enabled. It does this regardless of the
layout's completion `saved` flag. `EnvironmentPlacement._with_slot_overrides`
then translates the contact, hit rectangle, and label anchor together.
The following shipped values therefore take precedence in both main and WIP:

| Scenario | Slot | Main raw default | WIP experiment | Shipped override |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Convention | `local_floor_fixture_1` | 686,332 | 542,268 | 566,261 |

All slot names above have the `scenario.` prefix in the data. The House Calls
capacity blocker was resolved by giving that event a distinct slot in
`0575eb2d`, not by these raw scenario coordinates. Accepted overlap selection
and route checks from `69506f95` also remain intact.
