### Stability issue BTH-061-STAB-001: unrelated broadcasts exhaust a room scenario journal

- Severity: High; deterministic progression blocker after extended normal
  play in an active scenario room. No crash or save corruption was observed.
- Source checkout: `5764b15c157adc5ee1cd4a4022346d02b1f00266` with the batch's
  uncommitted public-input runner normalization.
- Seed: `BTH-061-STAB-B01-001`.
- Initial conditions: fresh isolated profile and no save, default content,
  exact seed entered through New Run, and the Clean route selected from public
  route support.

Exact reproduction:

1. At the source checkout above, run
   `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/rw06_2_ending_replay.ps1 -Ending clean -Seed BTH-061-STAB-B01-001 -ConfirmationOnly`.
2. Let the public-input route travel normally, enter and leave game surfaces,
   resolve interruptions, and preserve its mid-route Save -> quit -> relaunch
   -> Continue checkpoint.
3. In Grand Casino during the convention-crowd arrival phase, continue normal
   Blackjack play. On environment turn 99, play the visible K/5 hand and Hit;
   the drawn 10 produces a visible bust.
4. Attempt to settle the completed hand through the rendered Settle action or
   allow the autonomous settlement tick to run.

Expected: the Blackjack hand settles, its wager/economy result is applied,
and the Clean route remains playable.

Observed: the hand stayed in `settling`; the notice remained `Cards finish
moving before payout`, and the public failure message reported that the room
could not complete the action. Repeating Settle did not change public state.
The engine log contained no script error. The preserved save held scenario
`grand_casino_convention_crowd` in active `arrival`, environment turn 99, with
254 fact receipts and one visit receipt. A direct next-turn probe failed with
`scenario causal journal lifetime limit reached`.

Root cause: `RunState.scenario_enqueue_fact()` retained every public
game/town/heat broadcast for an active room sequence. The convention-crowd
sequence subscribes only to `game_result` and `travel_departed`, but unrelated
broadcasts consumed the strict 256-entry causal journal without creating a
replayable scenario cause.

Fix: accept but do not retain fact types outside the sequence's authored
`fact_subscriptions`; preflight a `world_boundary` receipt only when the active
sequence actually subscribes to it, and treat terminal sequence state as
inactive. The strict receipt budget, deterministic RNG, save schema, payouts,
and animation scheduling are unchanged.
