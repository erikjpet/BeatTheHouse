## The five headline pillars (lead with these)

### 1. The Grand Casino is alive now
Once a single finish-line room, now a set of connected, walkable spaces with
its own economy and cast:
- Split into local room environments — Main Floor, High-Limit, the Cage, the
  Back Room — each its own place you move through.
- A **chip economy** and a walkable **Cage** where Linda runs cashout, comps,
  and Players Card claims from behind the glass.
- A **living floor**: Rourke the pit boss and rival cheaters actually walk the
  room; staff rotate on a daily schedule; confrontations only happen where
  they should.
- **Players Card tiers** you claim from Linda in sequence, with prestige status
  and rewards that carry between runs.
- A **back-room showdown** presented as linked scenes that can escalate into a
  playable **heads-up duel with Rourke** — a phased fight with an outcome
  ladder, not a cutscene.
- Gated behind an **invite** you have to earn, so arriving there means
  something.

### 2. The interface got rebuilt from the ground up
The whole HUD and presentation layer was redesigned around the game as it
actually plays today:
- Structured HUD with a visible **run clock** — pressure is readable at a
  glance instead of buried in text.
- A dedicated **cheat dock**: risky actions live in their own space instead of
  hiding inside old action panels.
- Rebuilt room headers, menus, popups, conversations, sale screens, and the
  run report.
- A consistency sweep across the whole UI (shared design tokens, coherent
  spacing and type), plus small-screen and responsive fixes.

### 3. Scratch Tickets — the eighth game
A brand-new physical-lottery gambling surface:
- Bought from **scarce vending stock**; every three in-game hours the machine
  deterministically rolls 50% no ticket, 40% one, or 10% two, including
  elapsed-time catch-up. A seeded scalper can empty a visit and may or may not
  reveal the next restock time. Single tickets and multi-buys are supported.
- Outcome is **locked at purchase**, then revealed through a real drag-to-
  scratch machine with a layered scratch-off surface (background / icons /
  foil) and paper-foley feel.
- Seven fixed-outcome ticket games with rebalanced odds, wired into the
  collection payoff.

### 4. Video Poker, rebuilt as real cabinets
Rebuilt from scratch into three distinct machines:
- **Jacks or Better**, **Double Deuces** (2 hands), **Triple Double Bonus**
  (3 hands) — real multi-hand play where each hand draws from its own
  independent deck.
- Coin-level betting, a clear payout dashboard, improved hand readability, and
  distinct pixel-art cabinet fronts per machine.
- Its own skill-based **holdout cheat** minigame.

### 5. The world talks back
A full writing and character pass gave the game a voice:
- A **neo-noir voice bible** now drives every player-facing line; copy was
  rewritten across the game and event-choice buttons say what they *do* in
  character instead of announcing mechanics.
- Recurring characters speak from **line pools** (no more repeats): Rourke,
  Linda at the Cage, Sal the pawnbroker, the Crew, your brother-in-law, June
  the bartender, the Grand Casino host, and the patron archetypes.
- **Living conversations**: animated character models, multi-person scenes
  (Crew favors as a group, multi-lender talks), and the speaker shown in the
  dialogue overlay.
- A one-time **Dave** encounter on the night bus — a mystical, near-silent
  oracle who says the smartest thing you've heard, then goes quiet.

## Second-tier features (good for the devlog body / a thread)

### A guided first run
- The first run is now a **guided dialogue tutorial** led by "your pal" —
  deterministic scripted routes, a coach engine with always-dismissible tips,
  and a proper finale, instead of dry pop-ups.

### The meta layer got deeper
- **Meta-home header** simplified to what matters: current gold, home
  type/location, and the gold goal to tier up (items moved out of the header).
- Reorganized home item storage display; item pickups labeled correctly.
- **Sal's Pawn Shop** now has a resale shelf: recovered finds and forfeited
  collateral sit on the shelf, restocked once per successful run; pawn is now a
  tier-2 destination.
- **Collection bags** open with a case-style reveal reel pinned to the item you
  were granted; bag drops link into runs.
- Spatial inventory + item-interaction renovation; attribute **glyph/badge
  system** replaces prose stat text; transient item-discovery popups.

### New places to be
- New **beach** and **jazz club** environments; the Cage became a real
  walkable space; shops and the Jazz Club surface earlier.

### Sound
- **Adaptive jazz music system**: native adaptive tempo, harmony-safe
  arrangements, phrase-fill layering, and beat-synced outcome stingers.
- **SFX rework** to a casino-smooth procedural palette, then a realism pass so
  effects sound true to life (the phone actually sounds like a phone).
- Modernized web audio bridge.

### Systems
- **Open-hours / time system**: venues keep real hours, closing time reroutes
  and reschedules correctly, and the dead clock scaffolding was removed.
- **Skill-cheat pass**: passive casino cheats upgraded with real skill input,
  unified into the cheat dock, with locked interaction contracts.
- A **career stats** surface.

## Under the hood (for the "and a ton of fixes" closer)

- **Performance**: snapshot deep-copy root fix, per-frame allocation removed
  from hot paths, process fan-out guarded by screen state, and an idle-
  animation **liveness guard** so "idle optimizations" can never silently
  freeze table animation again.
- **Stability**: atomic run saves with backup recovery, content validation
  surfaced at boot, standalone Windows export resource-loading fix, and a batch
  of web travel/audio hang fixes.
- Deterministic, seeded simulation throughout (same seed → same run), verified
  by the determinism probe.

## Tone / framing notes

- Lead visual: the living Grand Casino floor or the Cage — it's the most novel
  and the most "wow."
- Angle for social: "the casino is alive now" + "an eighth game" are the two
  most tweetable hooks. The UI rebuild is the credibility beat for returning
  players.
- Keep the **responsible-play line** in every public post: *Beat the House is
  simulated gambling only — no real-money wagering, no cash prizes, no gambling
  monetization.*
- What's deliberately still out: no post-0.5 content teased here; keep scope
  honest to what ships.
