# Beat the House 0.6.0 — Complete Ending Runbook

This is the player and play-agent reference for completing each of the three
0.6.0 endings from a fresh standard run:

- **Clean:** earn Linda's Gold Players Card.
- **Cheat:** provoke Rourke and survive his back-room showdown.
- **Heist:** execute the Crew's Count plan and finish the getaway.

The instructions are **seed-independent**: follow the state shown by the game,
not a fixed list of clicks or a known card order. Start a separate run for each
ending; the first terminal outcome ends that run.

## What “any seed” can and cannot mean

These plans handle every currently known route state, interruption, deadline,
and recovery branch. Two random systems prevent an honest guarantee that every
possible seed can be won in finite time:

1. Clean and Rourke's duel use real Blackjack variance. A sufficiently bad
   shuffle can exhaust the available bankroll or duel stack even when every
   decision is correct.
2. Audit Night is rerolled on new game days, but has no pity counter or maximum
   wait. The Heist policy can keep advancing days until it appears, but cannot
   promise that it appears within a fixed number of days.

Accordingly, “any seed” here means one adaptive decision policy for all seeds,
with explicit retry and restart conditions. Reloading a save does not reroll a
deterministic card sequence or the current day's Grand Casino situation.

## Universal operating rules

- Treat the **rendered HUD, objective, enabled controls, and Linda's ledger** as
  authoritative. Do not assume a control is usable merely because its object is
  visible.
- Resolve every visible event or talk modal before attempting another action.
- If a needed object is not obvious in the room, open **More room actions**.
- If **Retry Pending** or **Retry** is shown after a sealed action, use it before
  making a new decision.
- Wait for dealing, settlement, travel, and dialogue presentation to finish.
  Do not double-submit an action while its controls are temporarily hidden.
- Use **Menu → Save** only at a stable decision boundary. **Continue** restores
  the active route, including Linda's card progress and Rourke's active duel.
- For ordinary Blackjack in the Clean and Cheat routes: **Hit below 17; Stand
  on 17 or above; then Settle**. The Heist has a different, shorter policy.
- A timed route advances on action boundaries. Once a Heist timer begins, take
  only the actions named in its section.

## Common opening: reach the Grand Casino

Use this opening for all three endings.

1. Start a standard run. **Menu → Skip Lessons** is optional.
2. Open the city map and follow enabled, revealed routes. Prefer unvisited
   casinos and higher-tier casinos until either **Kitty Cat Lounge** or
   **Delta Queen** is available.
3. At Kitty Cat or Delta Queen, open **High Roller Invitation** and choose
   **Take the invite**. This grants **$50** and reveals the Grand Casino.
4. Read the Grand Casino map card before travelling:
   - Travel from **Kitty Cat, Delta Queen, or the Beach is free**.
   - Travel from another origin has the displayed fare, normally $70.
   - Clean and Cheat should arrive with enough cash left to buy at least
     **50 Grand chips**. Required cash is therefore `displayed fare + $50`.
   - The Heist eventually supplies its own 24-chip live-play float, so it only
     needs the displayed fare.
5. If Delta Queen says the River Queen is away, each **Walk the Deck** action
   costs $10 and advances the travel lock. Use another free origin if one is
   available; otherwise reserve enough cash for the displayed number of walks.
6. If short of cash, use a visible positive-cash event or lender with terms you
   can carry, or earn the shortfall at an ordinary game. Do not gamble away the
   chip reserve. If no deterministic funding remains, restart instead of
   entering the ending route already insolvent.
7. At the Grand, accept Vivienne's welcome. Use the room routes to move between
   **Main Floor** and **Cage**. At the Cage, **Cashout Counter → Buy chips**.

Do not mix routes after this point. Never cheat on a Clean attempt. On a Heist
attempt, avoid cheating or provoking Rourke: a pending showdown can take over
the run, and Heist setup requires Heat at or below 55.

## Ending 1 — Clean: Linda's Gold Players Card

### Win condition

Earn and claim Bronze, Silver, and Gold in order. Accepting the Gold Card ends
the run with the public outcome **`players_card`**.

Each tier is a fresh segment. “Segment net” is the change in **cash + Grand
chips** since Linda issued the previous tier.

| Tier | Settled hands in this segment | Segment net | Segment peak Heat | Reward |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bronze | 1 | +$5 | ≤30 | 5 chips and one drink comp |
| Silver | 3 | +$15 | ≤30 | 10 chips, drink comp, suite rest, High Limit, one Linda look-away |
| Gold | 5 | +$30 | ≤30 | Gold Card and victory |

The standard route therefore requires at least nine settled hands and +$50
across three separate segments. A tier reward does **not** count toward the next
segment.

### Complete Clean plan

1. Arrive at the Grand with **no cheat evidence**, Heat no higher than Linda's
   displayed ceiling, and enough cash to buy at least 50 chips.
2. At the Cage, buy 50 chips. Enter **Main Floor → Blackjack**.
3. For every hand:
   - Do not Peek, count, take a cheat/advantage action, or make a side bet.
   - Select the wager with the policy below.
   - Deal, Hit below 17, Stand at 17 or above, and Settle.
   - Resolve any interruption with the Clean choices below.
4. Watch the visible Players Card objective. A tier is ready only when both its
   hand count and its segment-net requirement are met without the segment peak
   Heat exceeding its ceiling.
5. Claim immediately when ready:
   - Leave Blackjack and enter the Cage.
   - Open **Linda → Players Card review → Claim Players Card**.
   - Bronze: **Thank Linda**.
   - Silver: **Take Silver**.
   - Gold: **Accept Gold Card**. This is the Clean ending.
6. After Bronze or Silver, return to Main Floor Blackjack and begin the next
   fresh segment. Do not count earlier hands or rewards toward it.

### Adaptive wager policy

Use the table's published minimum and maximum rather than assuming fixed dollar
values.

1. Default to the table minimum.
2. On the first Silver hand, wager the table maximum.
3. After two consecutive losing hands, wager the table maximum.
4. Otherwise, if the previous hand won, the current segment net is positive,
   and no more than $12 remains, wager
   `min(table maximum, table minimum + $10)`.
5. Reset the consecutive-loss count after any non-loss.

This is the policy used by the successful release replay. It limits ordinary
exposure while giving slow segments a controlled push. It reduces risk; it
cannot eliminate Blackjack ruin.

### Clean interruption policy

| Interruption | Choose | Effect/reason |
|---|---|---|
| Eye in the Sky | **Change tables** | Costs $5 and removes 8 Heat |
| Counter Probe | **Count badly / Play Dumb** | Removes 2 Heat without taking an edge |
| Shift Change | **Let it pass / Ignore** | Avoids advantage evidence |

Recalculate the remaining segment net after any cash cost. Cooling current Heat
does not reduce the recorded segment peak.

### Clean recovery and restart rules

| State | Response |
|---|---|
| Chips low, cash available | Leave the table and buy 25 or 50 chips at the Cage. |
| No cash, ATM credit available | Borrow in $50 increments, up to a $500 marker. Repay before claiming. Interest is 5% at 3:00 AM. |
| Tier ready but claim disabled | Repay the Grand ATM marker; inspect Linda's segment peak Heat; confirm eligibility and that no showdown is pending. |
| Current Heat is high, peak still legal | After Silver, use its one suite rest for up to −12 Heat or rely on the one automatic Linda look-away for a non-cheat Heat gain of at most 5. |
| Segment peak exceeded the ceiling | Restart. Cooling current Heat cannot repair that segment's monotonic peak. |
| Any cheat evidence exists | Restart or finish the Cheat route. Clean eligibility is permanently closed for this run. |
| House Calls/showdown is pending | Finish the Cheat route or restart; Rourke takes priority over Clean. |
| Cash + chips cannot fund the table minimum and no credit remains | Restart; the run is economically dead. |

ATM debt freezes an already-earned claim rather than erasing it. Redeem chips
or use cash to repay the marker, then return to Linda.

### Clean decision loop for an agent

```text
reach Grand without cheating
buy chips until chips >= 50

for tier in [Bronze, Silver, Gold]:
    while Linda does not render this tier as claimable:
        if a modal is visible: resolve it with the Clean table
        if Retry is visible: Retry
        if chips < legal wager: buy chips or use the documented ATM recovery
        choose wager with the adaptive wager policy
        Deal
        while a hand decision is required:
            Hit if total < 17, otherwise Stand
        Settle

    go to Cage -> Linda -> Players Card -> Claim
    accept the one rendered tier choice

stop only when the VICTORY report says players_card
```

### Challenge and prestige variations

- Linda's rendered ledger overrides the standard numbers above.
- A carried prestige Gold Card does not skip Bronze or Silver and tightens the
  standard Heat ceiling from 30 to 25.
- **Card Sharp** adds $20 to the high-roller target, making the standard Gold
  segment target $50.

## Ending 2 — Cheat: survive Rourke's showdown

### Win condition

Create watched cheat pressure, answer **The House Calls**, and finish Rourke's
five-hand duel without the margin falling below −60. Either winning duel rung
ends the run with the public outcome **`showdown_survived`**.

### Preparation

1. Reach the Grand, buy at least 50 chips, and enter Main Floor Blackjack.
2. Be sober and avoid debt where practical. Alcohol and debt can reduce duel
   leverage by as much as 24.
3. Carry **none** of these classified items when House Calls begins. Leave them
   in home storage or dispose of them before provoking Rourke:

| Class | Items |
|---|---|
| Contraband | Marked Cards, Foil Sleeve, Weighted Keyring |
| Surveillance | X-Ray Glasses, Tab Detector, Tarot Card |

Zero classified items is the safe policy. The walk to Rourke allows only one
pocket change, so a loaded inventory cannot be repaired after House Calls.

### Build House Calls pressure

Repeat at minimum wager:

1. Deal.
2. When **Peek** is unavailable because its window is closed, use any enabled
   **Distraction**, then choose **Peek**.
3. Finish the hand with Hit below 17 and Stand at 17 or above; Settle.
4. Leave the table and inspect Main Floor for an enabled **The House Calls**.
5. If it is absent or visibly disabled, return to Blackjack and repeat.

Use these interruption choices:

| Interruption | Choose |
|---|---|
| Eye in the Sky | **Press Anyway** |
| Counter Probe | **Play Dumb** |
| Floor Staff Warning | **Talk Back** |
| Pit Boss Warning | **Walk Now** |

Do not divert into the Comped Suite or Rourke-noticed side events while building
pressure.

House Calls can become legitimate through any of these current conditions:

- Heat at least **85** with staff attention;
- Heat at least **95** regardless of attention; or
- at least **+$30 Grand Casino net winnings** plus cheat evidence, watched
  evidence, or Grand-visit Heat above 30.

Blackjack bars the player at 90 Heat. If that occurs, use **Back** and inspect
Main Floor: the watched-cheat route should already have made House Calls
pending. Never use the obsolete 70-Heat threshold from older route notes.

### The walk, pat-down, and interrogation

1. Open **The House Calls → Follow Rourke**.
2. On the walk:
   - With zero classified items, choose **Keep Everything**.
   - With exactly one, prefer **Crew: item** if offered; otherwise choose
     **Trash: item**. Trashing loses the item and has a 15% chance of +4 Heat.
   - With more than one, the safe recovery window has already been missed.
3. Pat-down consequences:

| What remains | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Nothing | Clean duel start |
| One contraband item without watched-cheat evidence | Minor confiscation |
| Any surveillance item, or two contraband items | All classified items confiscated, −18 player handicap, +5 forced ante |
| Three contraband items | Immediate loss |
| Watched cheat plus any remaining contraband | Immediate loss |

4. Choose **Take the Chair**.
5. For each of the three interrogation beats, choose **Take the Edge**. The
   evidence text changes, but this response policy does not.
6. Save at the stable boundary before duel hand one if desired.

### Rourke duel policy

There are five fixed-ante hands with separate duel stacks.

For each hand:

1. Read Rourke's tell before Deal, but make a call only after cards are dealt.
2. Use the exact tell mapping:

| Rendered tell | Action |
|---|---|
| “Rourke squares one slug twice.” | **Call the Stack** |
| “His thumb stays under the down card.” | **Call the Swap** |
| “Rourke gives nothing away.” | Make no call |

3. Never guess. A correct call transfers 18 duel chips, improving the margin by
   36. A false call costs 6, worsening it by 12.
4. Do **not** Peek, Count, Double, Split, or Surrender in the duel. A further
   cheat can carry up to 95% detection and an 18-chip penalty.
5. Hit below 17; Stand at 17 or above. If only one legal decision is enabled,
   use it. Settle the hand and repeat.

| Final state | Result |
|---|---|
| Margin ≥12 | **Walk Out Clean** — victory |
| Margin −60 through 11 | **Shown the Door** — victory |
| Margin below −60 | **Taken Out Back** — loss |
| Player duel stack reaches zero early | **Taken Out Back** — loss |
| Rourke's stack reaches zero early | Immediate victory |

### Cheat recovery and restart rules

- If House Calls is visible but disabled, keep building pressure; only the live
  enabled control is authoritative.
- If the table becomes barred, leave it and inspect Main Floor.
- If a saved back-room route is resumed, continue from the visible walk,
  pat-down, interrogation, or dealt-hand state. Do not restart the sequence.
- If **Retry Pending** appears, use it before the next decision.
- After **Taken Out Back**, reloading the same save does not change cards or
  tells. Use a genuinely earlier strategy branch or start a fresh run.
- Five losing hands with too few helpful tells can legitimately defeat correct
  play. That is the unavoidable every-seed limit for this ending.

### Cheat decision loop for an agent

```text
reach Grand; remove all classified items; buy chips until chips >= 50

while House Calls is not enabled:
    play one minimum-wager Blackjack hand
    open a Distraction when needed, then Peek
    Hit below 17; otherwise Stand; Settle
    resolve warnings with the Cheat interruption table
    leave Blackjack and inspect Main Floor

Follow Rourke -> Keep Everything -> Take the Chair
choose Take the Edge for all three interrogation beats

for each duel hand:
    Deal
    call Stack only for “squares one slug twice”
    call Swap only for “thumb stays under the down card”
    make no call for “gives nothing away”
    Hit below 17; otherwise Stand; Settle

stop only when the VICTORY report says showdown_survived
restart after taken_out_back if no earlier strategic branch remains
```

## Ending 3 — Heist: The Count

### Win condition

Read the Audit roster, lock **The Count**, complete its schedule and cart setup,
play the three live hands, and finish the marked getaway. The winning terminal
rungs are **Clean Sweep**, **Out Hot**, and **Somebody Got Pinched**.

The old Bishop grind is obsolete. The current release does **not** require
recruiting Bishop, raising Crew rank, or completing separate identity sessions.
A fresh run already satisfies The Count's Bishop rank and identity criteria.

### Protect the route before it starts

- Do not abandon Crew jobs, make false accusations, or dispute Crew payments.
  Avoid accepting unrelated timed Crew work before the heist.
- Do not provoke Rourke.
- Preserve a path home and enough cash for ordinary travel, sleep, and any dues
  that come due while waiting for Audit Night.

### Find Audit Night on any seed

1. Reach **Grand Casino → Main Floor** and inspect **Tonight** and the visible
   room actions.
2. If **The Audit Roster** is present, open it and choose **Read the shift**.
   This adds 3 Heat, permanently records the knowledge, and reveals Punchline.
3. If a Collector event follows, choose **Promise** and resolve any visible Crew
   favor normally.
4. If the roster is absent:
   - Leave the Grand and return home.
   - Use **Sleep** in 4–8 hour blocks until the HUD's **Day** changes. Ordinary
     travel/actions that cross midnight also work.
   - Re-enter Grand Main and inspect Tonight again.
   - Repeat until The Audit Roster appears.

Leaving and re-entering on the same day does not reroll the situation. Saving
and reloading the same day does not reroll it either. The Grand uses a `day:N`
operating cycle because it never closes.

The daily selection can also produce no situation, Gala, or Convention. Recent
scenario suppression helps variety, but there is no finite Audit guarantee.
Keep paying required home dues during a long wait and avoid optional debt or
jobs with deadlines.

Once **Read the shift** succeeds, that knowledge survives later situation
changes and Save/Continue. Audit Night no longer has to be active.

### Reach Punchline and lock The Count

1. Read the shift should reveal **Small Underground Casino / Punchline**.
2. If it is still absent, visit revealed locations in this order:
   **Back Alley → Gas Station Casino → Motel → Bar**. Look for
   **Parking Lot Tip → Follow the tip**. If Machine Jam chains at the Gas
   Station, choose **Wait**.
3. Travel to Small Underground Casino. On first entry choose
   **Side Door → Punchline password**; on later entries use **Enter Casino**.
4. Enter **Crew Back Room → Planning Table**.
5. Confirm **Lock The Count** is enabled and choose it.
6. Optional hidden-turn protection: use **Let the room talk**. If a witnessed
   warning enables **Change your place at the table**, take it. This hedges a
   detected Crew turn into a partial Out Hot finish instead of Night Closed.
   With no Crew grievances and no recruited non-Bishop crew, no hidden turn is
   expected.

This is a safe place to Save/Continue before starting the timed setup.

### Timed setup

1. At the Planning Table choose **Watch the schedule**.
2. Follow the marked, Crew-covered route directly to **Grand Casino Cage**.
3. Before the **12-action inclusive deadline**, with current Heat **≤55**, use
   **Hold Sightline once**. Older notes saying two holds are obsolete.
4. Successful schedule setup automatically starts the swap-cart route.
5. Move directly to **Grand Main**.
6. Before the cart's **14-action deadline**, use the visible floor handoff. If
   it is not obvious, open **More room actions → The Floor Contact: Hand Over
   The Package**.
7. A successful handoff automatically begins live play and grants exactly
   **24 Grand chips**.

Do not take detours during setup. The marked travel is paid by the Crew.

### Live Table: exact nine-action sequence

Complete the sequence immediately. Wager exactly **$8** on each hand; the legal
range is $8–$30, and the 24-chip grant covers three total losses.

1. **Live Table → Hold**.
2. Play and Settle exactly one $8 Blackjack hand; return to Live Table.
3. **Live Table → Sit**.
4. Play and Settle exactly one $8 hand; return to Live Table.
5. **Live Table → Dock**.
6. Play and Settle exactly one $8 hand; return to Live Table.
7. **Live Table → Take the exit**.

For these three hands, **Stand at the first legal decision**. Winning is not a
heist requirement; the shortest legal hand protects the nine-action inclusive
window. Do not Double, Split, take Insurance, or make a side bet.

If interrupted:

- Eye in the Sky: **Change table**.
- Blackjack Counter Probe: **Play Dumb**.

A hand played before its pending Bishop decision does not count. Return to Live
Table whenever a decision is waiting. **Early** and **Dump** continue the route
but each costs 12 score; use Hold and Sit for the best outcome. Sit deliberately
adds 6 Heat but does not itself reduce heist score. **Dock** is universally
available; the conditional Corridor exit is unnecessary.

### Getaway

1. **Take the exit** starts a marked delivery to **Delta Queen / River Queen**.
2. Follow only the marked, Crew-covered travel route.
3. Arrive within the **7-action dock deadline**. Do not take optional actions or
   jobs on the way. Arrival resolves the getaway and opens the terminal report.

### Heist outcomes and payout

The score starts at 100. The effective ladder is:

| Outcome | Effective score | Count payout | Run result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Clean Sweep | ≥80 | $1,150 ($900 + $250) | Victory |
| Out Hot | 55–79 | $900 | Victory |
| Somebody Got Pinched | <55 | $720 ($900 − $180) | Victory |
| Night Closed | Crew betrayal override | $0 | Loss |

A failed getaway subtracts 25 for ladder purposes but still normally reaches a
winning Heist rung. Important score losses include:

- wager outside $8–$30: −12;
- any one live hand gaining at least 15 Heat: −20 and Corridor lost;
- leaving Grand before all three hands: −15 and Corridor lost;
- live-play lateness: −20 and Corridor lost;
- Early or Dump: −12 each;
- failed getaway: −25 effective score.

### Heist recovery and restart rules

| State | Response |
|---|---|
| No Audit roster | Advance to the next game day and revisit Grand. Do not same-day reload. |
| Audit already read, situation changed | Continue; the knowledge is durable. |
| Schedule expired or Hold failed due to Heat >55 | Cool to ≤55, return to Planning Table, and choose Watch the schedule again. |
| Cart handoff expired | Return to Planning Table and choose Move the swap cart again; schedule knowledge remains complete. |
| A stale setup route is active before live play | **Fold the score** to clear it, then resume the locked plan. This charges `15 + 10 × completed setup keys`, capped to leave $1. |
| A Live Table decision is waiting | Return to Live Table and take that decision; extra hands played first do not count. |
| Live play is late or degraded | Continue through Dock and the getaway. A lower winning rung is still possible. |
| Getaway deadline missed | Complete the route; failure usually lowers the result to Out Hot rather than preventing the ending. |
| Live chips below $8 | The route is effectively dead. This only occurs if the intended float was spent on extra wagers, Double, Split, or side bets. |
| Night Closed | Restart and avoid Crew grievances, or take the visible hedge after a warning. |

**Fold the score** is available only before live play. Once the Play begins,
finish the route rather than looking for teardown.

### Heist decision loop for an agent

```text
reach Grand without provoking Rourke

while Audit Roster is absent:
    go home
    Sleep until the displayed Day changes
    return to Grand Main and inspect Tonight

Audit Roster -> Read the shift
reach Punchline -> Crew Back Room -> Planning Table -> Lock The Count
if Change your place at the table is enabled after a warning: take it

Watch the schedule
follow marked route -> Grand Cage -> Hold Sightline once (deadline 12, Heat <= 55)
go directly to Grand Main -> Hand Over The Package (deadline 14)

Live Table Hold -> one $8 hand, Stand at first legal decision, Settle
Live Table Sit  -> one $8 hand, Stand at first legal decision, Settle
Live Table Dock -> one $8 hand, Stand at first legal decision, Settle
Live Table Take the exit

follow only the marked route to Delta Queen (dock deadline 7)
stop only on a winning Heist terminal report
```
