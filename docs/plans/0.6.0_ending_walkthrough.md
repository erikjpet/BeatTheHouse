# Beat the House 0.6.0 — How to Win

There are three endings: **Clean**, **Cheat** and **Heist**. All three start by
getting into the Grand Casino.

## Basics

- **Your run ends** if you go broke, get stranded, get arrested, lose Rourke's
  duel, or abandon the run.
- **Heat** is how closely you're watched. Each ending treats it differently.
- **Tonight:** each venue rolls a nightly scenario that changes who's there and
  what you can do.
- **Can't find something?** Check **More room actions** at the top of the room.
- **Talk** to people by clicking them.
- **Save** with **Menu → Save**, and pick up again with **Continue**.
- **Skip the tutorial** with **Menu → Skip Lessons**.

## Getting into the Grand Casino

1. Visit the **Bar** and the **Gas Station Casino**, or visit **The Punchline**.
   That unlocks the **Kitty Cat Lounge** and the **Delta Queen**.
2. At either one, open the **High Roller Invitation** and choose **Take the
   invite**. You get **+$50**, and the Grand Casino appears on your map.
3. Travel to the Grand Casino from **Kitty Cat, Delta Queen or the Beach**.
   From those three the trip is free.
4. At the **Cage**, buy chips. The Grand Casino plays with chips.

## Clean: Linda's Players Card

Earn **Bronze → Silver → Gold** and claim each tier from **Linda at the Cage**.
The Gold review wins the run.

| Tier | Games since the last claim | Net win since the last claim | Heat | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 1 | +$5 | ≤ 30 | 5 chips, free drink |
| Silver | 3 | +$15 | ≤ 30 | 10 chips, free drink, suite rest, High-Limit Room |
| Gold | 5 | +$30 | ≤ 30 | **Win** |

- **Never cheat.** Any cheat evidence closes the card for the whole run.
- **Pay off any Grand Casino ATM marker** before you claim a tier.
- **The suite rest** from Silver lowers Heat, which helps if you're creeping
  toward 30.
- **About 200 actions.**

## Cheat: Rourke's Back Room

Provoke Rourke on purpose, survive his back room, and don't lose the duel badly.

**How to get called** (any one of these):
- win **$30** at the Grand Casino while cheating (or while Heat is above 30);
- reach **Heat 85** while staff are watching you;
- reach **Heat 95**.

**Steps:**
1. **Before you provoke him, drop risky items.** Contraband: Marked Cards, Foil
   Sleeve, Weighted Keyring. Surveillance: X-Ray Glasses, Tab Detector, Tarot
   Card.
2. **The House Calls:** choose **Follow Rourke**.
3. **The walk:** for each item, choose **Crew** (the Crew holds it, if you have
   Crew ties), **Trash** (it's lost, with a small Heat risk), or **Keep
   Everything**.
4. **The pat-down:**

   | Rourke finds | Result |
   |---|---|
   | Nothing | Clean start |
   | 1 contraband item | It's taken |
   | 2 contraband, or any surveillance item | Your duel starts 18 lower, plus a forced ante |
   | 3 contraband, or contraband after you were caught cheating | **You lose** |

5. **Take the Chair** and answer three questions. **Hold Steady** is best if
   you played clean. **Name Your People** leans on Linda and the Crew. **Take
   the Edge** is the strongest if you were caught cheating, but it makes Rourke
   more aggressive.
6. **The duel:** five hands of blackjack from your own duel stack. When Rourke
   **squares a slug twice**, choose **Call the Stack**. When **his thumb stays
   under the down card**, choose **Call the Swap**. A right call is +18, a
   wrong call −6.
7. **The result** depends on your margin: **+12 or more** is *Walk Out Clean*
   (win), **−60 or more** is *Shown the Door* (win), and **below −60** is
   *Taken Out Back* (loss).

**About 120 actions.**

## Heist: The Count

Read the audit, plan the score, set it up, play the table, and escape.

**You need Audit Night** at the Grand Casino. It doesn't happen every night.
**Don't anger the Crew** either: don't abandon their jobs or make false
accusations.

1. **At the Grand Casino on Audit Night,** open **The Audit Roster** and choose
   **Read the shift**. The game remembers it, and it reveals **The Punchline**.
   No roster tonight? Come back another night.
2. **Go to the Punchline's back room** (through the club), open the **Planning
   Table**, and choose **Lock The Count**.
3. **Planning Table → Watch the schedule.** At the **Grand Casino Cage**, use
   **Hold Sightline** within **12 actions**, with **Heat ≤ 55**. The Crew pays
   the travel.
4. **Take the swap cart**, which you're already carrying, to the **Main Floor**
   and complete the **handoff** within **14 actions**. The live play starts.
5. **The Live Table.** You get **24 chips**, enough for three $8 hands.
   Alternate decisions and hands in this exact order:

   **Hold → hand → Sit → hand → Dock → hand → Take the exit**

   - Hands played before **Hold** don't count.
   - *Early* and *Dump* also work, but they lower your score.
   - **Sit** adds +6 Heat.
   - The Live Table always shows the next step.
   - Everything has to happen within **9 actions**.
6. **The getaway:** take the cart to **Rook at the Delta Queen**, within **7
   actions** for the dock. Travel is free.
7. **The result:**

   | Result | |
   |---|---|
   | Clean Sweep | Best: $900 + $250 |
   | Out Hot | Win: $900 − $180 |
   | Somebody Got Pinched | Win |
   | Night Closed | Loss (a Crew member turned on you) |

   Lower Heat means a better result.

**About 165 actions.**
