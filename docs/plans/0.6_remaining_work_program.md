## Owner scope decisions (2026-08-25)

1. **Game depth parity and crew/world surface depth both ship in 0.6.** The
   depth standard applies to the whole game, not to craps/poker/pusher alone.
2. **SFX is agent work.** An SFX pass extends the shipped
   `data/audio/surface_sfx_manifest.json` pattern to the reworked surfaces.
   Production *music* remains the external audio-engineer delivery contract.

## The completion standard (inherited, binding on every row here)

From the owner's depth audit: a variation must be a unique playable sequence
that changes the space and what the player does in it. A game must not resolve
as a static control panel. A reward overlay, a metadata-only room change, or a
line of patron text is not depth. Every row below is measured against that bar,
and every gate row must be able to fail.

# Family 1 — Game depth parity (8 games + the duel)

`craps06_3` and `crew06_10` rework two of eleven games. `data/games/games.json`
ships `scratch_tickets`, `pull_tabs`, `slot`, `bar_dice`, `blackjack`,
`baccarat`, `craps`, `roulette`, `crew_draw_poker`, `video_poker`,
`coin_pusher`. Nine surfaces are unscheduled, and `depth06_1` would pass while
they still fail its own stated bar.

**Shared-file hazard:** `scripts/games/table_game_visuals.gd` (541 lines) draws
the room, table, dealer station, patrons, gaze focus, wager badges and round
timer for every table game. `scripts/ui/game_surface_canvas.gd` (1925 lines)
owns design space, hit regions, animation channels, liveness and perf counters
for all of them. Uncoordinated parallel content work would collide in both.
That is why `game06_1` exists.

### game06_1 — Table and machine ritual runtime

- **Objective:** the `env06_6` of games. Extend `GameModule` and the shared
  visual layer with the vocabulary the content rows need: multi-beat action
  phases with staged commitment (place / correct / confirm / resolve rather
  than one click), tactile pointer verbs on real board regions, dealer and
  patron actors with behavior states that react to money and heat, room objects
  that change state, and per-game energy that moves actors and objects rather
  than music values and a patron line.
- **Owns:** `scripts/core/game_module.gd`,
  `scripts/games/table_game_visuals.gd`, the shared parts of
  `scripts/ui/game_surface_canvas.gd`, a new ritual/actor contract and tests.
- **Depends on:** `craps06_3` — it is the first real consumer of a tactile
  throw/settlement ritual. Harvest its landed seam rather than inventing a
  rival one.
- **Unblocks:** `game06_2` … `game06_7`.
- **Acceptance themes:** no behavior change to any game that has not opted in;
  determinism unchanged; the liveness guard still green; parity and perf
  budgets hold; the contract is expressive enough to re-express the landed
  craps ritual without special-casing craps.

### game06_2 — Blackjack depth

- **Objective:** the flagship 7000-line game becomes a played table — chip
  placement and correction, dealer procedure with real beats, shoe and cut-card
  ritual, neighbours who play their own hands and react, pit and heat presence
  visible in the room instead of a suspicion number. Count-cheat and crew
  spotter integration must survive exactly.
- **Owns:** `scripts/games/blackjack.gd` and its tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`.
- **Risk note:** the most-integrated game in the project — Players Card,
  tutorial lessons, crew plays and heist honesty scoring all read it. Its
  prompt must enumerate every consumer before touching the settlement path.

### game06_3 — Baccarat and roulette depth

- **Objective:** baccarat's ceremony (shoe, card squeeze, banker/player ritual,
  commission handling) and roulette's physical wheel, ball and layout betting
  with chip placement on real felt regions, croupier procedure, and neighbour
  action.
- **Owns:** `scripts/games/baccarat.gd`, `scripts/games/roulette.gd`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`.

### game06_4 — Machine games depth (slot, video poker)

- **Objective:** the machine-side equivalent — cabinet body, credit ritual,
  handle and button feel, attract and denomination states, seat neighbours, a
  floor attendant on hand-pays, and the room reacting to a big win instead of a
  message. The coin pusher V3 cabinet is the reference for machine presence.
- **Owns:** `scripts/games/slot.gd`, `scripts/games/slots/*`,
  `scripts/games/video_poker.gd`, `video_poker_renderer.gd`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`.
- **Note:** the slot bonus watchdog per-frame deep-copy cost is a recorded
  historical regression. The prompt must forbid re-introducing per-frame copies.

### game06_5 — Counter games depth (scratch tickets, pull tabs)

- **Objective:** the counter ritual — clerk as an actor, selection from a real
  rack or roll, the physical scratch and peel already modelled extended into
  the room, redemption as a transaction with a person, and the clerk's
  attention as the visible form of heat. Preserves the shipped mask, region and
  foil renderers.
- **Owns:** `scripts/games/scratch_tickets.gd`, `pull_tabs.gd`, the ticket
  renderers, tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`.

### game06_6 — Bar dice depth

- **Objective:** the street register's own game. Opponent presence, cup and
  table handling, bar-room onlookers, wagers settled hand to hand, and the
  losing side reacting. Closest sibling to street craps — reuse `craps06_2`'s
  dispersal and teaching seams rather than duplicating them.
- **Owns:** `scripts/games/bar_dice.gd`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`, `craps06_3`.

### game06_7 — Rourke duel and Grand Casino showdown depth

- **Objective:** the endgame duel is the last thing a player sees on the
  showdown route and is currently the same panel as everything else. Four-phase
  duel staging, Rourke as a reacting actor, the room emptying and filling
  around the outcome ladder, and the crew heist route's alternate ending beat.
- **Owns:** `scripts/core/grand_casino_duel_model.gd`,
  `grand_casino_showdown_model.gd`, their surfaces and tests.
- **Depends on:** `game06_1`, `game06_2` (shares blackjack settlement).

### game06_8 — Games depth release gate

- **Objective:** independent exact-tree audit, sibling of `depth06_1`. Every
  game in `games.json` plays as a sequence; unlabeled contact sheets per game;
  RTP and EV re-verified per game; money conservation; cheat integration
  intact; Players Card, tutorial, crew play and heist consumers still correct.
- **Depends on:** `game06_2` … `game06_7`, and `depth06_1`.
- **Fails if:** any game still resolves as a control panel, any RTP moved
  outside its documented band, or any cheat or crew integration regressed.

# Family 2 — Crew and world surface depth

The flagship pillar is still a text menu. Streets deliveries, the Numbers desk,
the job board, coordinated plays, heist phases, recruitment encounters and
Police Sweep encounters all resolve through `EventModule` choice actions
(`crew_heist_table_choices()`, `crew_job_accept`, `crew_rook_ride`,
`crew_practice_rig` in `scripts/core/event_module.gd`). `env06_6` builds the
spatial, actor and objective machinery these need; nothing schedules them onto
it.

### world06_1 — Crew and world sequence adapter

- **Objective:** the bridge. Let crew and world systems author and run
  `env06_6` sequences instead of emitting choice lists, without duplicating the
  runtime and without leaking hidden Turn state into scene data. Defines who
  owns the `EventModule` seam so the content rows can run in parallel.
- **Owns:** the crew seams in `scripts/core/event_module.gd`, the adapter, tests.
- **Depends on:** `env06_6`, landed and reviewed.
- **Unblocks:** `world06_2` … `world06_6`.

### world06_2 — Streets: deliveries, holds, pursuit

- **Objective:** package runs, multi-stop routes, lookout holds and getaway
  chases become played sequences on real map nodes (post-`rework06_1`), with
  patrols, weather, crowds and the sweep painting the space, and the verbs
  (move, wait, duck, stash, ditch) acting on scene objects.
- **Owns:** `scripts/core/delivery_run_model.gd`, its surfaces and tests.
- **Depends on:** `world06_1`.

### world06_3 — The Numbers: book, desk, routes, rigging

- **Objective:** the book as a place with a person in it, slips as handled
  objects, staggered closes that are visible, runner routes as sequences, and
  both rig routes (crew fix, solo past-post) staged physically with their tells.
- **Owns:** `scripts/core/numbers_model.gd`, `data/crew/numbers.json`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `world06_1`.

### world06_4 — Back room: job board, jobs, recruitment

- **Objective:** Layer 3 as a room that changes with residency rotation, the
  job board as an object, the 13 shipped jobs staged per kind (`package_run`,
  `stake_horse`, `numbers_route`, `lookout_hold`, `collection`), the Practice
  Rig as apparatus, and the seven recruitment encounters as scenes.
- **Owns:** `crew_recruitment_model.gd`, the job seams in `crew_state_model.gd`,
  `data/crew/jobs.json`, `data/crew/recruitment.json`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `world06_1`.

### world06_5 — Coordinated plays and Police Sweep encounters

- **Objective:** the five plays become visible crew presence at the table
  (Switch on the rail, Velvet's distraction, the chip dump changing hands)
  using `game06_1`'s actor vocabulary; sweep encounters become staged street
  moments with real exits rather than a choice list.
- **Owns:** `crew_play_model.gd`, `police_sweep_model.gd`,
  `data/crew/plays.json`, tests.
- **Depends on:** `world06_1`, `game06_1`.

### world06_6 — Heist phases and the Turn confrontation

- **Objective:** both production plans staged as places and beats — planning
  table, dock versus corridor, the cage and interview beat, clean walk versus
  hot chase — and the unmarked confrontation as a scene. Hidden-state
  discipline is absolute: no scene datum may leak traitor identity.
- **Owns:** `crew_heist_model.gd`, `crew_turn_model.gd` surfaces, tests.
- **Depends on:** `world06_1`, `world06_2` (chase verbs), `crew06_10` (the
  poker tell clue channel).

### world06_7 — Crew and world depth release gate

- **Objective:** independent exact-tree audit of Family 2. Every crew and world
  interaction plays as a sequence; a hidden-information audit across every new
  scene surface; both heist plans and all three Cass endings replayed;
  save and revisit cannot double-fire a consequence.
- **Depends on:** `world06_2` … `world06_6`.

# Family 3 — Cross-cutting completion

### pusherv3_11 — Coin pusher V3 program closure audit

- **Why:** `pusherv3_4` was labelled closure, then rows 5-10 were appended
  reactively. Each was verified alone; no independent exact-tree audit covers
  the V3 machine as a program. The universal nudge, alarm and lockdown language
  did survive into the V3 solver, so this is an audit, not a rebuild.
- **Depends on:** `pusherv3_10`.
- **Also does:** retires the superseded `pusher06_0/1/3/4` rows and states which
  parts of `pusher06_2`'s presentation contract still bind.

### meta06_1 — Career, run report and meta surfacing of 0.6

- **Why:** `scripts/ui/career_stats_view_model.gd` hardcodes two victory routes
  (`players_card_cashout`, `showdown`). The `crew_heist` route added by
  `crew06_8` is tallied in the profile dictionary but has no row on the screen.
  Lifetime stats track no crew, numbers, scenario or pusher activity, and the
  run report references the heist and nothing else from the update.
- **Objective:** make 0.6 visible between runs — third victory route, crew
  standing outcome, nights and scenarios seen, numbers and pusher activity,
  honest `missing_stats` copy. Meta stays Players-Card-only per roadmap
  decision #1: this adds reporting, not a new cross-run progression system.

### teach06_2 — Teaching pass 2

- **Why:** `teach06_1` shipped seven `tip06_*` lessons (craps pass line, coin
  pusher, numbers book, crew standing and three more). Nothing teaches poker,
  deliveries, the sweep, the trust ladder, scenario objectives or rumour
  reading — and Families 1 and 2 invalidate the interaction those tips describe.
- **Objective:** re-audit all 63 lessons against the reworked surfaces, then add
  the missing once-only public-surface lessons under `teach06_1`'s accepted
  rules.
- **Depends on:** `depth06_1`, `game06_8`, `world06_7`.

### audio06_1 — Surface SFX pass

- **Why:** `data/audio/surface_sfx_manifest.json` contains exactly one profile —
  the coin pusher. Craps, poker, table games, scenarios and streets have no
  authored SFX map, and every reworked ritual needs one.
- **Objective:** extend the shipped manifest and event-class pattern to the
  reworked surfaces — deterministic, budgeted, Web and native parity clean, and
  respecting the existing mixer and settings. Production music stays external;
  the prompt writes the 0.6 delta into the audio-engineer handoff instead.
- **Depends on:** Families 1 and 2 landing their rituals.

### balance06_1 — Cross-system economy and difficulty audit

- **Why:** every prompt audited its own slice. Nobody has measured jobs plus
  deliveries plus numbers plus plays plus heist plus pusher EV plus craps as one
  bankroll.
- **Objective:** model and measure combined income and pressure across seeded
  full runs, per route and per playstyle; report inflation and dead ends;
  propose tuning rather than silently applying it. Runs before the playtest so
  the owner judges the design rather than the inflation.

### integ06_1 — Composition, migration and soak umbrella

- **Why:** each subsystem migrated its own 0.5 saves, and `depth06_1` composes
  one node at one moment. No matrix proves a real mid-0.5 save survives every
  0.6 subsystem together, and no full-run soak with everything active checks
  determinism and orphaned state.
- **Objective:** a 0.5-to-0.6 migration matrix, multi-system composition at
  shared nodes, and seeded full-run soaks to terminal on both platforms.

### perf06_1 — Performance and platform pass

- **Why:** the pusher physics and a spatial scenario runtime both landed after
  the last global pass, and Families 1 and 2 add actors and objects to every
  surface. `scripts/ui/performance_liveness_guard.gd` guards the recorded 4x
  idle-animation regression pattern, but no 0.6-wide budget exists.
- **Objective:** Web and low-end budgets, export size and load time, per-surface
  frame cost with the liveness counter-gate mandatory — an idle number of 0.000
  is a failure, not a pass.

### playtest06_2 — Playtest gate refresh

- **Why:** `playtest06_1` predates the depth program, `pusherv3_5`-`10` and
  everything in this document, and its dependency is literally "ALL other rows".
- **Objective:** re-scope it — a named seed list, a written playtest script that
  reaches every major path, a feedback capture format that turns findings into
  `fix06_*` rows, and an honest state-of-the-update report shape.

### polish06_0 — The second half of 0.6

- **Why:** the owner declared the post-playtest pass to be half the update; it
  is currently two parked prompts.
- **Objective:** give it a shape before it is needed — fix triage protocol,
  balance tuning pass, dead-code and docs cleanup, devlog plus publish copy plus
  trailer (0.5 had a separate plan per artefact), release checklist, version
  policy. Stays parked; it exists so the owner is not designing it while tired.

### board06_1 — Board hygiene

- Close the stale Chip Dump owner question: `data/crew/plays.json` ships model A
  (player-funded, `transfer_amount: 40`, `transfer_fee: 6`, `cash_to_chips`).
  The question is answered by shipped data.
- Retire the superseded `pusher06_*` rows into an archive section.
- Split the 170 KB board: the active table stays; the Discovery & Decision Log
  and Work Log move to dated companion files.
- Remove the ignored leftover `scripts/core/streets_run_model.gd.uid` — its
  `.gd` was deleted by `rework06_1`.
