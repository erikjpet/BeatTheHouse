## Changelog

0.5.0 rebuilds Beat the House around the parts players touch the most: the end
of the run, the casino floor, the scratch-ticket counter, and the whole user
interface.

### Highlights

- A larger Grand Casino endgame with a main floor, high-limit room, walkable
  Cage, sequential Players Card claims, chips economy, and a playable Rourke
  back-room duel.
- Scratch Tickets join the game roster as the eighth playable gambling surface,
  with themed tickets, scarce vending stock, fixed-at-purchase outcomes, and
  scratch-off presentation.
- A full interface rebuild: new design tokens, structured HUD, wallet and
  pressure readouts, interactive clock, room headers, dedicated cheat dock,
  autosized popups, animated conversations, and redesigned menus.
- A smoother onboarding arc, including the Cage gift shop tutorial.
- Sal's Pawn Shop now has a resale shelf that can stock recovered run-end finds.
- Collection bags open with a cosmetic reveal reel pinned to the item already
  granted by the bag roll.
- Spatial inventory and item interactions were rebuilt for cleaner storage,
  container, loadout, sale, and action flows.
- Stability fixes from the 0.5 playtest queue, including web travel/audio and
  chip-input hardening.

## Itch.io Page Copy

### Short Description

A single-player casino roguelike about debt, heat, crooked tables, and one last
climb to the Grand Casino.

### Long Description

▌ BEAT THE HOUSE

Beat the House is a casino roguelike about surviving one bad run through the
wrong side of town.

Start nearly broke, scrape together a bankroll, dodge heat from the house,
manage debts to dangerous people, and push your luck toward the Grand Casino.
Every run is seeded and deterministic, built from procedural venues, games,
items, services, lenders, events, and the hour-by-hour pressure of the city
clock. The odds are never kind, but every risk has a readable upside and a
consequence waiting behind it.

▌THE GRAND CASINO IS BIGGER NOW

The 0.5.0 release turns the Grand Casino into a real destination. The floor is
split across connected rooms with a walkable Cage, Linda behind the counter,
chip handling, Players Card claims, and a back-room sequence that can end in a
heads-up duel with Rourke. You can still try to leave clean, but the house now
has more ways to notice you.

▌SCRATCH, PEEL, AND CHASE A LONGSHOT

Scratch Tickets are now a full game in the roster. Vending-machine stock is
scarce, each ticket locks its outcome at purchase, and scratching is pure
presentation. Tickets use themed art, per-symbol reveal regions, readable
results, and prize logic tied to their own rules.

▌A NEW INTERFACE FOR THE WHOLE RUN

The interface has been rebuilt for the 0.5.0 flow. The HUD now presents wallet
movement, heat, alcohol pressure, status icons, and the current run clock at a
glance. Rooms get their own title headers. Risky actions live in a dedicated
cheat dock. Popups resize to their content, conversations animate with clearer
speaker presentation, and menus/sale screens have a new visual pass.

▌BUILD A LIFE BETWEEN RUNS

Between runs, walk your home, open collection bags, manage storage, choose
loadouts, sell spare finds, and check Sal's Pawn Shop. Sal's shelf can now
stock run-end finds for later purchase, and bag openings use a case-style reel
that lands on the item already rolled and granted.

▌PLAY FULL-SIMULATION CASINO GAMES

Eight playable casino games sit inside the run:

Scratch Tickets: scarce vending stock, fixed-outcome purchases, themed ticket
mechanics, and scratch-off reveals.

Pull Tabs: finite deals, sealed windows, staged tab-peel reveals, and item
interactions.

Slots: generated machines, feature rounds, jackpots, autoplay, and pinball
bonus play.

Blackjack: shoe play with hit, stand, split, double, side bets, count pressure,
and risky hole-card peeks.

Roulette: inside and outside bets, chip placement, rebet, recent numbers, and
live wheel resolution.

Baccarat: mini-baccarat with Player, Banker, Tie, pair bets, shoe state, roads,
squeeze reveals, and commission.

Video Poker: multiple variants with bet, hold, draw, double-up, and advantage
tools.

Bar Dice: Ship, Captain, Crew rounds with keep/reroll decisions against bar
patrons.

▌CHEAT WHEN YOU DARE

Cheating is optional, but temptation is part of the run. Each game has a risky
advantage action, now surfaced through the rebuilt cheat dock. Alcohol dulls
your timing. Contraband sharpens it. Push too hard and heat climbs toward
police capture, house pressure, or a back-room reckoning.

---

Beat the House is a roguelike about simulated gambling. It is not a real-money
gambling product. There is no real-money play, no purchasable in-game currency,
no cash prizes, and no gambling monetization.
