1. Remove the `audit_night` injection. The heist policy must discover and use
   only player-visible legal opportunities created by production state.
2. Remove every temporary `crew_presence` rewrite. Crew contact, route, rank,
   job, capability, status, reward, and settlement must remain production-owned.
3. Build policies exclusively from a player-visible observation projection and
   route every selected action through public production entry points.
4. Add paired-observer tests proving that equal public knowledge with different
   hidden state produces byte-equal policy inputs and available actions until
   the accepted reveal boundary. Privileged diagnostics may exist only in a
   separate output never consumed by a policy.
5. Record an exact opportunity denominator for each system and style, separating
   inaccessible, visible-but-not-selected, selected, accepted, rejected, and
   settled opportunities.
6. Replace the single generic action-cap label with exact censor reasons,
   action count, terminal status, and remaining player-visible reachable routes.
7. Add immutable resumable shards. Each shard must bind exact commit/tree,
   engine/plugin identity, input-manifest hash, policy hash, schema hash, seed
   range, command, timestamps, exit code, and output hash. Aggregation must
   reject gaps, overlaps, duplicates, changed manifests, and mixed provenance.
8. Preserve the required frozen sample: eight styles × 64 seeds × 208 maximum
   actions, plus exactly three pusher strata × 200,000 accepted paid-origin
   drops. Attempts, refusals, rejects, opening stock, bonus/child inserts,
   retries, and retained physical value remain separately reconciled.
