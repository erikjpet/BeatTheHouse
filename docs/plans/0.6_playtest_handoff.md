## Known limitations and open findings

- **Visual consequences and object identity are rough.** Scenario state changes
  can be clear in the selected panel without changing the object's glyph.
  Hundreds of objects still share fallback glyphs and may depend on their
  labels for identification. The owner must decide the bar for unique art and
  raster-visible state changes before parked `env06_9` begins.
- **Punchline art remains provisional.** L1/L3 use registered art slots backed
  by the existing underground raster plus distinct code-rendered layouts.
- **One of nine mouse routes retained a delivery finding.** Seed `-008` reached
  populated rooms and traveled, but a visible physical-delivery handoff did not
  clear the active delivery run, so the broader QA script stopped before game,
  save and victory coverage. Eight other seeds passed the complete loop. This
  is not the dead-panel/departure-refusal defect, but the owner may see a
  delivery that needs a follow-up interaction or fails to clear.
- **Maximal-composition revisit caveat.** After a Crew-rank mutation, a retained
  integration diagnostic can reject a previously visited scenario because its
  semantic digest predates that mutation. It did not reproduce in the eight
  passing mouse runs, but a heavily composed revisit may refuse and should be
  reported with the seed and room.
- **Windows export wrapper false negative.** The full export produced the valid
  executable, but the PowerShell wrapper captured Godot's console output along
  with its zero exit code and reported the combined value as a failure. The
  already-built artifact then passed the wrapper's native/runtime/folder checks
  through `-SkipExport -NoPackage`. This is tooling noise, not a game boot
  failure, and should be fixed during refinement.
- A broader, explicitly out-of-scope Systems attempt timed out at its aggregate
  boundary after exposing seven inherited Family Loan T4.7 failures. The new
  Crew-favor focused contract is green; this broad result was neither hidden nor
  used to expand the fast path.
- Balance is deliberately unjudged. Economy pacing, distributions and Coin
  Pusher long-run EV still need the owner's feel notes before final tuning.
- Voice/register is deliberately unjudged. Text polish and the final register
  pass wait until player-visible strings stop moving.

No known player-facing 0.6 feature is deliberately left registered-but-inert.
Act 2 remains outside this update, and data-labelled future game variations are
not part of the current playable promise.
