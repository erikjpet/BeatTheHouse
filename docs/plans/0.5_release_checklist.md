## Included Scope

- Grand Casino rework: three connected rooms, chips-and-Cage economy, the
  walkable Cage with Linda, Bronze/Silver/Gold Players Card claim flow, the
  back-room showdown sequence, and the playable heads-up Rourke duel.
- Players Card lifecycle: run-end reward handling, carried-card prestige rules,
  failure loss rules, and run-report/meta integration.
- Dialogue-guided first-night tutorial led by Pal and the Grand Casino Host,
  including the Apartment/X-ray start, Corner Store/family loan, optional Gas
  Casino pull-tab route, Underground Blackjack/Peek/counting/Heat, invitation,
  Cage gift shop/chips, Bronze claim, recap, and normal-run handoff.
- Scratch Tickets as the eighth game, including the seven-ticket roster,
  ticket-stock scarcity, fixed-at-purchase outcomes, scratch presentation, and
  themed ticket art layers.
- Bag opening reel presentation for collection bags: cosmetic reel reveal
  pinned to the already-granted item, followed by full contents showcase.
- Sal's Pawn Shop resale shelf with run-end stock, shelf inspection, price
  quoting, purchase confirmation, and meta save integration.
- Spatial inventory and item-interaction renovation for storage, containers,
  loadouts, item actions, sale views, and responsive interaction surfaces.
- Full UI 0.5 redesign: design tokens, rebuilt start/settings/game-test menus,
  structured HUD with wallet/heat/drunk/status/clock presentation, per-room
  title headers, dedicated cheat dock, autosized popups, animated conversation
  UI, and redesigned sale showcase.
- Post-overhaul conversation and event-popup polish: multi-person lender
  conversations, Crew group conversations, portraits, and room/event timing
  updates tied to the environment clock.
- Web/stability work after the 0.5 feature pass, including store-travel freeze
  fixes, web audio stall fixes, and input hardening found during play.
