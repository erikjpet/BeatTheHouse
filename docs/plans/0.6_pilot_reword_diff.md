## Notes

- `the_collector` and `crew_favor_delivery` appear in **both** the corner store
  and grand casino pools, so both were written register-neutral rather than
  Street. Same for the `house_drink` service.
- `parking_lot_tip` choice text "Opens the Small Underground Casino route."
  was announcing its own mechanic, which 0.5 bans as prose. Reworded to
  "The way underground opens." which keeps the information without the
  spec-sheet voice.
- `comped_suite_offer` originally said "The drink is free" inside a *suite*
  event. Changed to "The room is free" so the line matches its own event.
- `eye_in_the_sky` and `lights_out_return` already used "the ceiling" and
  "arithmetic" before this pass. The renamed-noun lexicon was drawn from
  existing data, not imposed on it.
- `whale_sighting` keeps "He takes the light" in a House venue. "Takes" is a
  Street verb only when a *machine* is the subject; here the subject is the
  whale, a person.
