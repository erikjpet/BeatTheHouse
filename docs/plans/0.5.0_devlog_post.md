# Beat the House 0.5.0 Devlog Post

Status: **READY TO POST — 0.5.0 released on 2026-08-12.**

## Title

log #5 — the house changed

## Body

0.5 is the largest update to Beat the House so far.

This update was less about adding one isolated feature and more about making the
whole run feel connected. The Grand Casino is now a real destination with its
own rooms, chips, Players Cards, and a complete Rourke showdown. Scratch
Tickets join the game as a new way to build—or lose—a bankroll, while the new
interface makes time, Heat, conversations, travel, and risky decisions easier
to understand.

The First Night tutorial has also been rebuilt around actually playing the
game. It now walks through debt, items, travel, Blackjack, cheating, casino
chips, and the first Players Card without separating those lessons from the
world they belong to.

A large part of this update happened under the surface. Long-running saves,
large inventories, discarded tickets, casino animation, and background slot
machines have all received a major performance pass. The result is a smoother
game that can carry much larger and stranger runs without slowing down.

## Closing

0.5 is now the official playtest baseline. Thank you to everyone who helped
find the slow runs, broken animations, confusing lessons, and small rough edges
that only appear after sitting with a game for a while.

The house is bigger, sharper, and paying closer attention.

Full release notes and Web/Windows downloads:
https://github.com/erikjpet/BeatTheHouse/releases/tag/v0.5.0

Beat the House contains simulated gambling only. There is no real-money
wagering, no cash prizes, and no gambling monetization.
