## Identity-specific proof

- [ ] Delivery Day physically reroutes aisles and branches sorted versus
  inspected/ignored cartons into different shelf and clerk/service states.
- [ ] Lotto Fever stages a growing queue, disputed place/number handling, stock
  pressure, and cleared/angry/celebration layouts.
- [ ] Aftermath uses boarded glass, evidence, restricted path, nervous clerk,
  and moving plainclothes actor; recovery/flag/ignore produces distinct access.
- [ ] Dead Shift makes circuit restoration and deliberate darkness a staged
  visibility/privacy trade with different surveillance and access aftermath.
- [ ] Inventory Night closes sections, follows a physical discrepancy trail,
  and reopens, quarantines, or rearranges stock.
- [ ] Street Craps integrates the accepted craps ritual through public facts:
  assemble/read ring, shoot, respond to lookout, then continue/relocate/disperse
  with exactly-once stake recovery and persistent chalk/actors.
- [ ] Cruiser Parked uses real sightline/cover and a diversion choice; cruiser
  reposition/exit or watched route persists without reading sweep private state.
- [ ] Fence Night moves lots and buyers among stations; authenticate/broker/walk
  changes stall/exit control.
- [ ] Nothing Moving uses three physical traces and follow/erase branches that
  change exits, rumor access, and returning actors.
- [ ] Estate Lot moves objects through appraisal/display/hold and branches
  provenance into sold/quarantined/returned physical lots.
- [ ] Serial Check restricts inventory with inspection/hold zones and traces one
  serial through records/objects; disclose/withdraw/wait changes access/actors.
- [ ] Sal's Mood visibly moves Sal and gates concrete shop tasks, ending in
  reopened counter, private appraisal, or closed shutters.

## Package conflicts
