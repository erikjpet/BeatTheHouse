Four Baccarat findings remain binding under Options A and B:

1. **Pre-window reduced-motion concealment.** Reduced motion currently projects
   squeeze progress as complete before the authored squeeze time window and
   before the player crosses the reveal action boundary. The card must remain
   concealed before both boundaries in every motion mode. Reduced motion may
   shorten/complete the presentation only after the equivalent authorized
   reveal verb.
2. **Partial pointer resume.** Beginning another pointer gesture resets progress
   to zero. A released incomplete squeeze that reports it will hold must resume
   monotonically from its prior progress; begin/move/end/cancel cannot silently
   discard or manufacture progress.
3. **Hand-bound mid-squeeze persistence.** Save, exit and revisit during a
   partial squeeze lose the binding between progress and the exact hand/card.
   Persisted state must bind hand identity, fixed authoritative card/slot,
   progress, phase and one-shot/settlement status so restore cannot change the
   card, reset or replay the hand, or double-settle.
4. **Material actor/object/energy rendering.** The renderer consumes projection
   mostly as a diagnostic status strip. The dealer/callers/neighbours/security,
   shoe/road boards/discard tray/felt and energy/rail/attention states must
   materially change the visible scene or an interactable. Text, metadata,
   music or a status strip alone is not material behavior.

These are acceptance findings, not implementation instructions to weaken or
special-case the frozen ritual contract. All other requirements in the full
prompt remain open until proven on an immutable production-composed head.
