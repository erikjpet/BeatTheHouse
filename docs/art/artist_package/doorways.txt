DOORWAYS

WHAT THESE ARE
When the player travels, the game shows the inside of the next place
through its open front door. Each picture here is that glimpse.

WHAT TO MAKE
Paint over each picture and save it with the same file name.
Keep each picture's exact size. Most are 1024 x 1536 (tall). The list
below gives every size.

RULES
- The inside of the room only. No door, no door frame, no outside walls,
  no street. The game draws the door around it.
- No people, no words, no logos.
- No transparency. Fill the whole picture.
- Match the colors of that room's finished painting.
- The middle matters most. The edges may be cut off by the door.
