Delta Queen

MOOD
A riverboat casino. Brass, practiced courtesy, and no easy way off. Mid-level stakes, not a brown tavern.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
White and cream painted woodwork with magenta edges, honey windows, brass rails, red wood, deep teal river outside. Green table felt.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- Card tables
- Windows looking out at the river
- Brass rails
- The boarding door

DO NOT ADD
- The outside of the boat or a paddlewheel
- Water under the floor where people walk
