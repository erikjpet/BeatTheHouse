Motel Lobby

MOOD
A tired motel lobby where people rest, rent rooms and pass through. Quiet, pink, temporary. Must look different from the private motel room.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Faded rose and plum plaster, a brick base, teal metal trim, honey-colored curtains and lamps, a pink VACANCY light. Worn cheap wood, faded fabric, an old TV.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- A table
- A merchandise counter
- A door
- A small phone shelf
- An old TV

DO NOT ADD
- A clerk, a caller or a card dealer
- Items for sale

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- The merchandise counter on the right looks like it floats. Give it a full body down to the floor.
