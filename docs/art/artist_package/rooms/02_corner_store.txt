Corner Store

MOOD
An ordinary small corner shop at night. Fluorescent, quiet, watchful. A real working shop, not a copy of the gas station.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Purple brick, cyan and magenta stripes, cool blue glass, mint-white fluorescent lights, cheap cream counter tops and steel.
