Grand Casino Back Room

MOOD
Rourke's private office. Still, polite, final. Not a horror room.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Immaculate dark plum and wood, small brass and gold details, one warm lamp. The lowest energy of the Grand rooms.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- Two tables
- A desk
- An OFFICE sign

DO NOT ADD
- A crowd, trophies, big screens or a dramatic spotlight

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- The two tables look like upright green panels. Draw real tables with flat felt tops and bases on the floor.
- Sign glows show as solid rectangles. A glow should follow the letters.
