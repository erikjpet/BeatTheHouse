Beach

MOOD
An open-air beach at night for recovering between runs. Salt, neon, quiet.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Warm tawny sand, an amber weathered wooden boardwalk, cyan surf and foam, a small pink pier in the distance.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- A towel stall
- The boardwalk
- The sea and sky

DO NOT ADD
- A resort, a casino, a pool or tables
- Shiny reflections on dry sand

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- Neon bars float in the sky with nothing holding them. Turn them into lights on the distant pier, or remove them.
- The umbrella pole must be one straight pole standing in the sand, not cutting into the boardwalk.
