Punchline Comedy Club

MOOD
A tired small comedy club with a two-drink minimum and a plain side door. Thin, hopeful, performing.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Purple brick, amber marquee bulbs, red wood, a plain door, small cyan and magenta trim. A worn wine-colored curtain.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- A small stage with a microphone stand
- Four round tables
- A plain side door

DO NOT ADD

- Any sign of the hidden casino downstairs
