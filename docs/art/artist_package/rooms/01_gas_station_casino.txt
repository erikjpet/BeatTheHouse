Gas Station Casino

MOOD
A roadside gas station shop turned into a cheap little casino. Bright, cheap and watched. Scratch tickets, machines, road dust. Not run-down, not luxury.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Plum and magenta brick walls, a magenta-and-cyan sign band, warm amber tube lights, cool blue glass. A big window looking out at the highway. A gold-barred cashier cage.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- The highway window on the left

- The cashier cage on the right, with gold bars
- Tube lights along the ceiling
