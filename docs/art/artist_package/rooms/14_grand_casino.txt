Grand Casino Main Floor

MOOD
The big final casino. Polished, watched, expensive. Gracious but in control.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Plum and magenta panels, gold framing, warm chandelier lights, small cyan edges. Green felt, gold and plum kept clearly separate.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- Machine bays along the wall
- Two card tables
- A host station
- Doors

DO NOT ADD
- Machines or staff (the game draws them)
- A treasure-hall look or an arcade look

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- The two tables look like upright green panels. Draw real tables: a flat felt top seen at an angle, a dark wood or plum side below, and a base on the floor.
- Sign glows show as solid yellow rectangles. A glow should follow the letters and the lamp shapes.
