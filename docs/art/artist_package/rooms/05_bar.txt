Bar

MOOD
A worn neighborhood dive bar. Noisy, warm, local gossip. Not a fancy lounge.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Deep teal painted wood panels, burgundy wood, amber rippled glass, hot-pink neon. A scarred red-brown bar counter, green pool table felt.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- The long bar counter with a mirror and bottle shelves behind it
- A pool table
