Kitty Cat Lounge

MOOD
A velvet lounge with champagne and stage lights. Polite and attentive, with a hook. Classy, not tacky.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Wine and burgundy velvet, pink scalloped edges with gold trim, honey lamps, cat and champagne signs.
