Punchline Hidden Casino

MOOD
The secret basement casino under the comedy club. Tight, pink and dangerous. Low voices, better tables.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
The same plum brick and red wood as the club upstairs, with low ceiling beams, green table felt and amber string lights. A faint pink and cyan glow from the stairs.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- Two card tables
- A low beamed ceiling
- A cart and a door

DO NOT ADD
- Games, dealers or players (the game draws them)
- A fancy poker room look

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- The two green tables look like upright panels with nothing holding them up. Draw real tables: a flat felt top, a wooden side below, and legs or a base on the floor.
