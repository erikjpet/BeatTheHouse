Pawn Shop

MOOD
Sal's pawn shop. Quiet, barred windows, fluorescent light. A shop of repairs, second-hand goods and bad decisions. Not a luxury jeweler.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Violet and plum brick, cold cyan light through barred glass, old yellow-gold sign frames. Mismatched old wood, dusty glass, dull metal, security mesh.
