Back Alley

MOOD
A real service alley behind buildings. Wet, electric, a place for shady deals. Not a movie villain's lair.

COLORS AND MATERIALS
Dark purple uneven brick, one bare amber light bulb, cyan and magenta light from a passage, wet stone and asphalt, soft reflections.

MUST BE IN THE PICTURE
- A folding table in the middle
- A hanging lamp on a wire
- A crate stand on the right
- The ground line where the wall meets the street

DO NOT ADD
- Items for sale on the table or crates (the game places them)
- Hooded people or a seller
- Rain lines painted into the picture (rain is animated separately)

FIX FROM THE CURRENT VERSION
- The crate stand on the right looks like it floats. Give it a solid body that sits on the ground.
